01Evidence trail

80% install recordings. Fewer than 5% watch them.

Session-replay telemetry and merchant interviews agreed: more than 80% of Shopify store owners install a session recording tool — Hotjar, Lucky Orange — and fewer than 5% watch recordings regularly.

The reason was operational fatigue, and merchants said it plainly: "I don't have time to watch 500 session recordings to figure out what to fix or test."

02Problem statement
Shopify merchants lose up to 97% of site traffic because session-recording tools flood them with raw replays instead of prioritised, dollar-quantified friction points with an actionable fix.

04How we arrived at the solution

Aggregate friction across sessions, price it, attach the clip.

The session-analysis pipeline watches user movement — hesitation on size guides, scroll patterns, script lag — and aggregates friction patterns across sessions rather than surfacing single replays.

Each behavioural anomaly is translated into a dollar-impact estimate and backed by a 15-second replay clip, so the path runs from detection to a deployed code fix without a merchant ever opening the raw recordings.

05What happened

Fixes shipped four times faster, and the lift held.

Attaching proof-of-friction video to developer instructions cut time-to-implementation for critical fixes by 75%. Done-For-You clients saw a validated +20% average lift in store-wide CVR within eight weeks, and the Director tier held 92% retention.
