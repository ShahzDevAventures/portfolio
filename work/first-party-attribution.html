01Evidence trail

A 35% gap, and 15 hours a week of spreadsheets.

After the iOS 14 privacy changes, client ad data showed a 35% average variance between Meta- and Google-reported ROAS and actual Shopify payouts. Merchants were scaling campaigns on numbers that did not match their bank account.

The second problem was organic. Merchants told us they spent 15+ hours a week trying to line up influencer post timing against sales spikes by hand, and still could not say which creator earned what.

02Problem statement
E-commerce brands cannot accurately attribute orders across fragmented ad networks and organic creator channels, so marketing budgets are misallocated and influencer ROI goes unmeasured.
03Tradeoffs

Deterministic, not probabilistic.

Chose

A first-party tracking script with custom UTM parameters

Over

Machine-learning statistical modelling

Gave up

A small share of edge-case coverage that a model would have inferred.

Got

100% auditability. Every attributed order traces to a real click, which is what made merchants trust the number over the ad platform's.

Controls inside the report, not next to it.

Chose

Live campaign controls (budgets, pause) in the attribution view

Over

A read-only dashboard

Gave up

Simplicity, and some scope. Write access to ad platforms is more to build and maintain.

Got

The decision and the action in the same place, which is where the time savings came from.

04How we arrived at the solution

Visitor journeys first, then the same architecture for creators.

We built individual visitor-journey tracking that mapped every touchpoint from first click to checkout, on the merchant's own domain, so nothing depended on a platform's pixel.

PostTrack extended that architecture: merchants issue unique link parameters for influencer bio-links, and organic social gets the same attribution clarity as paid campaigns, side by side in one view.

05What happened

Brands moved a quarter of their spend.

With variance under 3%, brands reallocated 24% of previously misattributed ad spend into organic and creator channels that had been under-credited, and dropped the weekly spreadsheet.
