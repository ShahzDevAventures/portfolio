01Evidence trail

Four to six tabs, and 42% missing their margin.

Session replays showed media buyers opening four to six browser tabs every morning — Facebook Ads Manager, TikTok Ads, Google Ads, Shopify — just to evaluate daily ROAS and adjust budgets.

42% of merchants reported missing target profit margins because they reacted late to overspending ads scattered across those tools. The cost was not the tabs; it was the delay.

02Problem statement
Growth managers fail to hold target profitability because cross-channel monitoring and campaign adjustments require constant platform-hopping, which slows reaction time and wears people down.
03Tradeoffs

Quick actions, not a campaign builder.

Chose

Lightweight controls: scale a daily budget, toggle an ad on or off

Over

A full campaign creation suite

Gave up

Complex ad setup. Campaigns are still built in the native platforms.

Got

Focus on the one high-frequency job: the daily performance decision.

One vocabulary across three networks.

Chose

Normalised metrics — blended ROAS and CPA — under one framework

Over

Channel-specific terminology and reporting

Gave up

Network-specific nuance that power users occasionally wanted.

Got

An apples-to-apples comparison a merchant can read in one pass.

04How we arrived at the solution

Shadow the morning routine, then build for its highest-frequency decision.

We shadowed e-commerce media buyers through their daily workflow to isolate the decisions they made most often: scale, hold, or cut.

Cross-channel reporting was standardised into a single performance dashboard that also flags the gap between platform-reported figures and true Shopify sales, with one-click adjustments available where the number is read.

05What happened

The dashboard became the place people started their day.

Daily ad optimisation dropped from 45 minutes to 15. Faster reactions saved an average of $1,200 a month in unprofitable spend per merchant, and a 78% DAU/MAU ratio showed the tool had become the primary workspace rather than a report people checked.
