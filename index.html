02 About / Operator's note

I build products the way operators do — metrics in one hand, the team in the other.

Most recently CEO at Sirge. Now looking for a senior product seat to go deep on one product instead of running the whole org.

I've spent the last four years running every function. I'm looking for the opposite — going deep on one product at a growth-stage company where the operating bar is already high.
