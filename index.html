I build products the way operators do — metrics in one hand, the team in the other.

07 Approach / 2026 operating principles
P / 01

AI is the floor, not the ceiling.

Generic AI output is the starting line. The role of a senior PM in 2026 is to set taste, constraints, and direction so the model produces a useful first draft — and to know exactly where humans need to take over.

Receipts: Anti-slop AI pipeline →
P / 02

Proof of work over proof of role.

Resumes describe what someone was allowed to do; portfolios show what they actually shipped. The site you're using right now is part of the interview.

Receipts: this site, the skills section →
P / 03

Build the system, not the heroics.

Single-points-of-failure are an org smell. The right move is almost never working harder — it's designing the operating layer so the team can ship without you, then doing it again.
